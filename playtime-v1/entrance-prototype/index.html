CANQI

CANQI is an installation artist and spatial designer whose practice also includes experimental theatre and digital media. With a background in architecture and environmental design, he works with physical structures, moving images, sound, performance, and interactive systems. His projects explore how people experience space and how technology, nature, and social relationships shape everyday life.

CANQI approaches space as a medium for storytelling. He is interested in how an audience’s movement, attention, and participation can become part of a work. His approach connects artistic ideas with the practical use of materials and technology, creating situations that viewers can enter, explore, and interpret in their own ways.

SCENE REFERENCE Jacques Tati, dir., PlayTime (France/Italy, 1967). French theatrical release: 16 December 1967.
